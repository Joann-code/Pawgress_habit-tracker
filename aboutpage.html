Our Story

Behind the Paws

Pawgress was born from a simple realization: the tools we use to improve our lives shouldn't make us feel anxious. We believe in steady, quiet growth over high pressure streaks.

The Creator

A Passion for Purpose

Hello I'm Joan, the mind behind Pawgress. As a developer with a deep interest in building functional, data-driven web applications, I wanted to create something that balanced technical rigor with a human centric philosophy.

This website was built as a personal portfolio piece to explore the intersection of sophisticated state management, thoughtful user experience, and interactive design. My goal is to show that data doesn't have to be cold, it can be the foundation for nurturing personal change.
